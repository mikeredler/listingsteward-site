A 5-minute self-check for your listing

Search your business name on Google Maps from your phone and check these:

  1. 01

    Hours

    Are today's hours right, including weekends and holidays? A listing that says "Closed" during a power problem sends the call elsewhere.

  2. 02

    Category and services

    Is "Electrician" the primary category, and are the jobs you want more of (panels, EV chargers, generators) listed as services?

  3. 03

    Reviews

    When did your last review come in, and did you reply to it? Unanswered 1- to 3-star reviews are the ones new customers read most carefully.

  4. 04

    Description and photos

    Does your description say you're licensed and insured and name the towns you serve? Do photos show your real trucks, team, and finished work?

Common electrician listing problems we fix

  • A home address showing publicly when the business is really a service-area business

  • Duplicate listings left over from an old phone number, an old address, or a previous business name
  • A website button that points to a dead page or a Facebook page that has gone quiet
  • Years-old reviews with no replies, which make an active company look like it's slowed down

Questions, answered

I work from home. Will my address show on Google?

It doesn't have to. If customers don't come to your address, Google's guidelines say to hide it and list the towns you serve instead. We set up your service area (Google allows up to 20 areas) so you show up where you work without publishing your home address.

Should my listing say "Open 24 hours"?

Only if someone really answers the phone at 2 a.m. If you offer emergency calls but not round-the-clock staffing, we set your real hours and make the emergency line clear in your description and posts, so callers aren't let down.

Can you mention my Massachusetts license?

Yes. We can say you're licensed and insured in your description and posts, using the exact details you give us. We never add credentials you haven't confirmed.

Is this the same as Google Local Services Ads?

No. Local Services Ads are paid ads you buy from Google. Your Business Profile is the free listing on Google Maps and Search. We manage the free profile; we don't sell or run ads.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add ListingSteward as a Manager on your Business Profile. You stay the owner and can remove us anytime.

Do you guarantee a #1 ranking?

No, and nobody honestly can. We guarantee the work: accurate info, weekly posts, and every review answered within 24 hours.

Do you write or buy reviews?

Never. Fake reviews break Google's policies and can get a listing suspended. We help you ask real customers.
