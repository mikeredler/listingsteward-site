Questions, answered

Why did Google suspend my Business Profile?

Google usually doesn't say exactly. Common triggers include a business name with extra keywords, an address that doesn't meet Google's guidelines (such as a virtual office or a P.O. box), a service-area business showing a home address, several profiles for one business, or big edits made all at once.

How do I appeal a suspension?

Google handles reinstatement requests through its official Business Profile appeals tool. You submit the profile and supporting evidence, then wait for a decision by email. There is no phone number that skips that process.

What evidence should I gather?

Proof that the business is real and operates as listed: a business license or registration, a utility bill or lease in the business name, photos of signage, vehicles, or storefront, and a website that matches the profile details.

Can you guarantee reinstatement?

No. Only Google decides. We guarantee the work: an honest diagnosis, cleanup of guideline problems, a well-documented appeal, and a clear status update.

Will I lose my reviews?

Reviews are generally hidden while a profile is suspended. If the profile is reinstated, they usually come back with it.

How do I avoid another suspension?

Keep the name exactly as it appears on your signage, use an eligible address or service area, avoid duplicate profiles, and make changes gradually. That is what Monthly Management does every month.
