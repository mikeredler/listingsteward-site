Questions, answered

What is the free sample fix?

We check your public Google listing and your website by hand, then write fixes you can paste in yourself: a business description, a services list and Google posts. It's free and yours to keep, with no obligation to buy.

Do you need my Google password?

No. If you hire us, you add ListingSteward as a Manager on your Business Profile. You stay the owner and can remove us anytime.

Do you guarantee a #1 ranking?

No, and nobody honestly can. We guarantee the work: accurate info, weekly posts, and every review answered within 24 hours.

Never. Fake reviews break Google's policies and can get a listing suspended. We help you ask real customers.
