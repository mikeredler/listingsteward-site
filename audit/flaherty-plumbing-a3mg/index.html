What's holding it back

  • Your website says Flaherty "is now proud to offer professional electrical services throughout the MetroWest area," but none of your 7 Google categories is Electrician, so the listing is unlikely to show up for "electrician Framingham" searches.
  • Your 4.6 rating trails Sostilio and Sons (4.8) and Shahood Plumbing & Heating (4.8), both near you in the same search.
  • Two of your categories are retail ones (Furnace store, Furnace parts supplier). If you don't run a walk-in store, they can dilute what Google thinks you do.

Fixes, in priority order

  1. Add Electrician (and Electrical installation service) as categories, and add your electrical services to the Services list.
  2. Ask every recent customer for a review with your direct link. More fresh 5-star reviews is the only way to move a 4.6 average.
  3. Review the retail categories and keep only the ones that match how customers actually buy from you.
  4. Post weekly about electrical work so Google sees the new service line is active.
