What's holding it back

  • A second Roofers Edge & Siding listing in Natick has a different phone number ((774) 286-1240), different hours (9–5 weekdays vs "Open 24 hours") and no reviews. Customers who land on it see zero reviews instead of your 103.
  • Your main listing says "Open 24 hours" every day. If the office keeps normal hours, that can lead to after-hours calls going unanswered and frustrated reviews.
  • The main listing includes "Door shop" and "Snow removal service" among its categories, which can dilute what Google thinks you do.

Fixes, in priority order

  1. Confirm whether the second listing is yours. If it is a duplicate, ask Google to merge or remove it; if it is a separate office, give it real hours and ask for reviews on it.
  2. Set real hours on the main listing (or "Open 24 hours" only if a person answers the phone 24/7).
  3. Review the categories and keep the ones that match your actual services.
  4. Keep posting project photos.
