Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. In plain English.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

ListingSteward ("we," "us") is a small Google Business Profile management service based in Boston, Massachusetts. This policy explains, in plain English, what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us or reply to one of our emails, we receive your name, email address, business name, and whatever you choose to include in your message.
  • Google Business Profile access. If you hire us, you add ListingSteward as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. That gives us access to your profile's details, reviews, photos, posts, and performance insights. We never ask for your Google password.
  • Onboarding details. Information you give us to do the work, such as your services, service area, hours, and photos.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored by us. We receive limited records from Stripe, such as your name, email, billing address, what you bought, and payment status.

Our free listing grader runs entirely in your browser. The answers you select are not sent to us or stored.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and provide the services you buy.
  • To manage and improve your Google Business Profile on your behalf, and to send you reports.
  • To bill you, keep business and tax records, and prevent fraud.
  • To send occasional business emails about our services. Every marketing email includes a way to opt out.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Stripe, for payment processing and receipts.
  • Zoho Mail, which hosts our email.
  • Google, because the work we do happens inside your Google Business Profile, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations. When you click a payment button, you go to a Stripe checkout page, and Stripe may use cookies under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep email correspondence and client records for as long as we work together, and then for up to 7 years for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. When you remove us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, our access ends immediately. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of email. Reply "unsubscribe" to any of our emails, or email us, and we will stop sending you marketing emails.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.
  • Remove our access. You can remove ListingSteward as a Manager on your Google Business Profile at any time.

We will respond to requests within a reasonable time, usually within a few business days.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by Massachusetts law.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell current clients by email.
