    Questions, answered

    Is the Google Business Profile grader free?

    Yes. It takes about 2 minutes, there's no signup, and your answers aren't sent anywhere. Only the free sample fix form sends us your business name, town and email.

    How is my score worked out?

    You answer 12 yes/no questions about your listing, such as categories, hours, services, photos, posts and review replies. Each answer is weighted, and you get a score out of 100 plus your top 3 fixes.

    Is the grader score the same as the free sample fix?

    No. The grader is a self-check, so the score depends on your answers. For the free sample fix we check your real public listing and website by hand and write a description, services list and posts you can paste in. Request it with the form on this page.

    What if my score is low?

    Start with the top 3 fixes it shows you, and request your free sample fix. If you'd rather we do it all, the $149 Cleanup is finished within 5 business days, and you keep ownership of your profile.

    Do you need my Google password?

    No. You add ListingSteward as a Manager on your Business Profile. You stay the owner and can remove us anytime.
