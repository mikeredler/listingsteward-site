Questions, answered

Who can become a partner?

Web designers, bookkeepers, accountants, insurance agents, trade suppliers, and anyone who works with local service businesses.

How much do I earn?

20% of the monthly fee for each client you refer, for their first 12 months as a client. At $199/month that's about $39.80/month per client, up to about $478 per client.

When am I paid?

Monthly, for each referred client's paid invoices from the prior month.

No. Make an intro or share your link. We do the audit, the pitch, and the work.
